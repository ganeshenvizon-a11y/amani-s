AMANI WEBSITE CONTENT STRUCTURE

Premium Restaurant Website Copy and Content Direction

Purpose: This document contains the complete page-by-page content structure for the Amani restaurant website. It follows the calm, atmosphere-first storytelling rhythm documented in the Amani Design, Motion and Imagery System while using entirely original copy written for Amani.

Primary goal: Help visitors feel the restaurant, understand the food, trust the experience, and confidently reserve a table, plan a gathering, explore the menu, or visit the location.

1. CONTENT PRINCIPLES

1.1 Core brand idea

Amani is where food, warmth, and belonging meet.

Amani should not communicate like a conventional restaurant that leads with offers, discounts, or an oversized menu. The website should first establish a feeling of place and then guide the visitor toward practical actions.

The content journey should feel like:

Arrival → Atmosphere → Food → Philosophy → People → Trust → Invitation

1.2 Brand promise

Amani creates thoughtful dining experiences through expressive food, genuine hospitality, and spaces that invite people to slow down and connect.

1.3 Writing tone

The writing must be:

Warm, composed, and human

Premium without sounding formal or distant

Sensory without becoming vague or poetic everywhere

Concise enough to scan comfortably

Confident without making exaggerated claims

Focused on gathering, taste, feeling, and hospitality

Avoid:

“Best restaurant in the city”

“Unforgettable experience” repeated across sections

Excessive luxury terminology

Long ingredient-heavy paragraphs outside the menu

Generic phrases such as “food made with love”

Aggressive booking language

Too many exclamation marks

1.4 Primary message pillars

Food with character

Every dish should feel considered, expressive, and connected to a clear culinary point of view.

Hospitality with warmth

Guests should feel welcomed, understood, and cared for without unnecessary ceremony.

A place for togetherness

Amani should feel equally relevant for an unplanned meal, a quiet evening, a family table, or a meaningful celebration.

Moments worth returning to

The experience should be described through small, believable moments rather than oversized claims.

14. FINAL CONTENT RULES

Keep hero copy under 25 words before supporting text.

Keep most section introductions between 25 and 60 words.

Use one clear primary action per section.

Do not repeat “experience,” “thoughtful,” “warm,” or “gather” in every section.

Use sensory language where it adds meaning, not as decoration.

Separate atmosphere-led copy from practical information.

Keep prices, hours, policies, menu content, and capacities data-driven.

Use real reviews, real team stories, and real photography wherever possible.

Never fabricate awards, heritage, sourcing relationships, or dietary guarantees.

On mobile, place essential practical information before long editorial passages.

Use “Gatherings” for the branded page but include “private dining,” “events,” and “group bookings” naturally for search clarity.

Keep CTA labels consistent across the site.

Write image alt text for the information conveyed, not for visual decoration or keyword stuffing.

Review all content with the founder, chef, restaurant manager, and operations team before publishing.

Preserve Amani's own identity; use the reference site's narrative discipline, not its wording.
